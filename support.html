About ClassFlow Support

ClassFlow: Academic Manager is designed to help students organize their academic activities, manage their workload, and monitor their academic performance. This page provides basic guidance and troubleshooting information for ClassFlow users.

Getting Started

1. Accessing ClassFlow

Open ClassFlow on your compatible Apple device. You may continue as a guest or use an available sign-in option.

2. Managing Courses

Use the Courses section to add and organize your academic courses and related information.

3. Organizing Your Schedule

Use Schedule and Calendar to organize class schedules, important dates, and academic activities.

4. Managing Assignments

Add assignments, monitor deadlines, and track pending and completed academic tasks.

5. Organizing Notes and Materials

Create academic notes, organize study materials, and keep learning resources accessible.

6. Monitoring Academic Performance

Use the Academics section to record and monitor academic grades and performance information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use ClassFlow without signing in?

Yes. ClassFlow provides a guest access option.

Why is some AI functionality unavailable?

Certain on-device AI capabilities require a compatible Apple device and supported operating system. Availability also depends on Apple's supported hardware and software requirements.

Why is the weather information unavailable?

Check your internet connection and ensure that ClassFlow has the necessary location permission if you want location-based weather information.

What should I do if the application stops responding?

Close and reopen ClassFlow. Check whether an app update is available. If the problem continues, contact support with a description of the issue.

Need Additional Assistance?
