Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to ClassFlow.

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClassFlow: Academic Manager ("ClassFlow," "the App," "we," or "our") handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, ClassFlow may handle the following information:

  • Account information: Information made available through Apple Sign-In or Google Sign-In, such as your account identifier, name, and email address where provided by the authentication provider.
  • Academic information: Courses, class schedules, assignments, notes, study materials, grades, and other academic information that you enter into the App.
  • Location information: Your device's location may be accessed if you grant permission for location-based weather information.
  • App preferences: Settings and preferences needed to operate the application.

2. How Information Is Stored

ClassFlow is designed with local-first data storage. Academic information entered into the App is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

The current app architecture does not use a ClassFlow-operated cloud database to synchronize academic records. Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it or remove the application, subject to your device's backup and storage settings.

3. Account Authentication

ClassFlow provides guest access and supported authentication options, including Apple Sign-In and Google Sign-In.

When you choose an authentication provider, that provider processes information according to its own privacy policy. ClassFlow uses the authentication information needed to manage your app session and account experience.

4. Location and Weather Information

ClassFlow may request location permission to provide location-based weather information.

Location access is optional. You can manage or revoke location permission through your device settings. If permission is not granted, some location-dependent weather features may be unavailable.

Weather information is provided through Apple's WeatherKit service and is subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Artificial Intelligence Features

ClassFlow includes AI-related features intended to assist with academic organization and learning activities.

Certain AI capabilities use Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework on supported devices and operating systems. When processing is performed on-device, the relevant input is processed by the device's supported model.

AI feature availability may depend on device compatibility, operating system version, language, and Apple's supported features.

6. Third-Party Services

ClassFlow integrates or may rely on third-party services to provide selected features, including:

  • Apple authentication services
  • Google Sign-In services
  • Apple WeatherKit
  • Apple Foundation Models, where supported

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and service terms. Their practices are not controlled by ClassFlow.

7. Information Sharing

ClassFlow does not operate its own cloud database for academic record synchronization in the current app architecture. Information may nevertheless be processed by third-party providers when you choose to use their integrated services.

We do not claim that information handled by third-party providers is outside their respective privacy practices.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Academic information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App, subject to your device's backup and storage settings.

You may manage locally stored academic information through the available features in ClassFlow. Removing the App may remove its local data, but information included in device backups may be retained according to your backup settings.

For account-related questions or privacy requests, contact the developer through the official ClassFlow support page.

9. Children's Privacy

ClassFlow is an academic organization application. It is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated online database. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of third-party authentication and other connected services.

10. Security

We rely on Apple's platform security features and the security mechanisms provided by integrated authentication services. Users should keep their devices and operating systems updated and protect access to their devices.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ClassFlow introduces new features, changes its services, or modifies how information is handled. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact Information

For questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please visit the official ClassFlow Support page.
